# Development instructions

Read MASTER-CONTRACT.md and harness/state.json. Determine the active authorization and writable owner from persisted state and the external human authorization record. Verify prerequisites and scope, run real checks and retain evidence, and stop at the authorized boundary. A freshly exported distribution requires operator activation. See harness/README.md.

Formal skill-task outcomes follow `harness/outcomes.md`: the authorized collector registers and validates the JSON artifact and retains its signed receipt. Missing or invalid outcomes block acceptance and handoff. Ordinary conversation remains free-form.
